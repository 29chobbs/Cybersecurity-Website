Hackers

What are they?

Hackers are individuals who exploit computer systems and networks, either maliciously or ethically, and cybersecurity aims to protect against these threats.


To protect against hackers, it is essential to implement robust cybersecurity measures, such as:
Regularly updating software and operating systems to apply security patches.



Using strong, unique passwords and enabling two-factor authentication.

Installing and maintaining reliable antivirus software.

Educating employees about phishing and social engineering tactics.

Securing networks with firewalls and encryption.
Understanding the different types of hackers and their motivations is crucial for developing effective cybersecurity strategies and fostering a safer digital environment.
